DiabAgent combines the Agentic Physiological Control framework, the DiabetSim simulator, and the SweetBench benchmark to study sustained glucose control in simulated type 1 diabetes scenarios.

- Paper: [DiabAgent: Toward Agentic Physiological Control](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03601)
- Code: [THU-CST-SAST/DiabAgent](https://github.com/THU-CST-SAST/DiabAgent)

This is a research platform for simulation, not a clinical treatment system.
